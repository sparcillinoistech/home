Mentoring program

Three mentoring perspectives. One connected community.

SPARC's three-pronged approach is designed to support academic progress, campus transition, career preparation, and professional growth.

01 / Faculty

Academic guidance and mentorship

Faculty mentors provide academic guidance and mentorship, helping scholars navigate academic choices, degree requirements, and their overall degree journey. Faculty development is intended to support scholars from a range of backgrounds.

02 / Peer

Campus and cohort support

Peer mentors help scholars navigate academic, co-curricular, extracurricular, and professional issues, discover campus resources, and connect with the student community.

03 / Alumni & industry

Career coaching

Alumni and industry mentors can act as career coaches and professional role models, offering networking and field-specific guidance.

Digital hub: An interactive digital hub brings together official communications through Canvas and informal discussion, information-sharing, and resource-sharing through a Discord server. Program updates and event schedules may also be shared through these channels.
