Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Answers based on the current official SPARC page and the program content brief. Verify details with the program team when needed.

Who is eligible?

Academically talented students from low-income families who qualify for Pell Grants through FAFSA and enter a listed SPARC program may be considered. The official page describes high school graduates and community-college transfer students.

Do I need a separate SPARC application?

The program brief says there is no separate SPARC application. Students complete regular Illinois Tech admissions and FAFSA.

What is the timeline?

The program follows the regular university admissions timeline for high school and transfer students. Check Illinois Tech admissions for current deadlines.

Can current students apply?

The program brief says the scholarship is awarded to incoming students only. Existing scholars may be eligible for renewal if program requirements are met.

Can community-college transfer students be considered?

Yes. Qualified transfer students are automatically considered as outlined by the program.

How long can support last?

The program brief describes four years for a bachelor's degree and five years for a combined bachelor's/master's pathway, subject to requirements. Confirm current terms with SPARC.

Can a scholar change majors?

The program brief says a change is possible if the new major is a qualified SPARC major. Confirm the approved list with the program team.

What can scholarship funds cover?

The official SPARC page says awards cover tuition and fees and may also help defray room and board, textbooks, transportation, and laptops.
