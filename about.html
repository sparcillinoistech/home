About the program

Supporting pathways into computing and STEM.

SPARC stands for Supporting Pathways for Advancing Readiness in Computing. The program supports academically talented students from low-income families pursuing qualified STEM degrees at Illinois Tech.

What SPARC is designed to provide

A connected student experience combining scholarship support with mentoring and opportunities to participate in academic, professional, and community activities.

Financial support

NSF-funded scholarship support can cover tuition and fees and may be used toward other education-related costs described by the program.

Academic guidance

Faculty mentors provide academic guidance and help scholars navigate their degree pathway.

Career and community

Peer support, alumni/industry mentoring, events, and experiential opportunities help scholars build connections.

Important: Scholarship eligibility and award details can change. Check the official Illinois Tech SPARC page for current information.
