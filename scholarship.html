Scholarship & eligibility

Support for eligible students entering STEM fields.

High school graduates and community-college transfer students from low-income families who demonstrate academic merit may be automatically considered.

Bachelor's degree

Up to $15,000 annually

Official Illinois Tech information lists scholarship support of up to $15,000 annually toward a bachelor's degree for eligible students.

Continued study

Up to $20,000 annually

Eligible students may receive support to continue studies to a master's degree. Confirm award conditions and combined-degree eligibility with the program.

How consideration works

There is no separate SPARC application according to the program brief. Complete the university admissions process and FAFSA.

STEP 01

Apply to Illinois Tech

Submit the standard application for admission.

STEP 02

Complete FAFSA

Eligible students must qualify for Pell Grants through FAFSA as stated by Illinois Tech.

STEP 03

Program review

Eligible incoming applications are reviewed by the SPARC team.

STEP 04

Selection

Top candidates may be interviewed before offers are made, according to the program brief.

Before applying: Verify current eligibility, award amounts, deadlines, and renewal conditions on the official page or with the SPARC team.
